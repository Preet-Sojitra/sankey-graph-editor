Mouse & keyboard
  • Drag a node or ribbon to move it. Ribbon ends touching a node move with the node.
  • Shift+click adds to the selection; Shift+drag on empty canvas draws a selection box.
  • Node: drag the green dot to pull out a new ribbon; blue dots resize. Double-click to edit its label.
  • Ribbon: orange dots move the ends (they snap to node edges and stack under neighbouring ribbons; hold Alt to stop snapping). Blue squares change the width. Purple diamonds bend the curve freely (Shift keeps the tangent horizontal).
  • Labels can be dragged to fine-tune their position.
  • Pan: drag empty canvas, hold Space, or use the middle mouse button. Zoom: mouse wheel / pinch, + −, 0 = fit.
  • Arrows nudge (Shift = 10) · Del delete · Esc deselect / cancel drag
  • Ctrl/⌘ + Z undo · Shift+Z/Y redo · D duplicate · C/X/V copy/cut/paste · A select all · S save
  • N new node · R new ribbon · ? toggle this help

Your work is autosaved in this browser. Use Save to keep a .json copy you can reopen or share.

About

Sankey Graph Editor is a free, open-source Sankey diagram maker that runs entirely in your browser. Unlike tools that lay out the diagram for you, it lets you place every node and shape every ribbon by hand, which is what you need for figures in research papers, theses and slides. Export clean vector SVG or PDF (or PNG), with your paper's font. Nothing is uploaded; your work stays on your computer.
